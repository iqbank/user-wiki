# Logging In to IQBank

## Logging in

To log in to your IQBank account, follow these steps:

1. Visit the [IQBank login page](https://iqbank.teach.cs.toronto.edu/), go to the home page, and click the **Login** button.

    ![IQBank home page]({{ "/assets/images/getting-started/home-page.png" | relative_url }})
    {: .guide-image .guide-image--medium }

2. Enter your email and password.

    -  Enter your email address (eg: `firstname.lastname@mail.utoronto.ca`)
    - Enter your temporary password. 
        - UofT students: This is your UTORid followed by your student number and an exclamation mark (!), with the first letter capitalized.

    ![Login page]({{ "/assets/images/getting-started/login.png" | relative_url }})
    {: .guide-image .guide-image--small }

3. Click the **Sign In** button. Upon successful login, you will be directed to the [Explore Course](https://iqbank.teach.cs.toronto.edu/courses) page where you can access the course(s) that your account is enrolled in. 

    ![Explore Courses page]({{ "/assets/images/getting-started/explore-courses.png" | relative_url }})
    {: .guide-image .guide-image--medium }

If you are having trouble logging in, please [contact]({{ "/contact/" | relative_url }}) the IQBank team.

## Changing your one-time password

After logging in to your account for the first time, you should change your one-time password to a secure password of your choice. For the instructions, please see [Change/Reset Password]({{ "/account-management/change-password.html" | relative_url }}).
