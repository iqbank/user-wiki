## Viewing the Course Homepage
As noted in the [Logging In to IQBank]({{ "/getting-started/logging-in-to-iqbank" | relative_url }}) section, you will be brought to the [Explore Course](https://iqbank.teach.cs.toronto.edu/courses) page upon successful login.

Locate the specific course you want to view, and click the **Repo** button to view the **Course home page**.

![Viewing course home page]({{ "/assets/images/using-iqbank/course-homepage.png" | relative_url }})
{: .guide-image .guide-image--medium }

*Course home page for UofT's CSC369 course*
{: .guide-caption }

This page provides an overview of the collections and other resources available in the course. Collections are sets of practice assessment questions designed to test knowledge across several topics. 

From this page, you can:

- **Browse an entire collection:** Click on the **Browse Collection** button to open the course’s Collections page and view all available collection sets.
- **Explore trending questions:** Use the **Hot Questions** section to view individual questions based on recent activity.
- **Browse questions by topic:** Click on one of the topic tags displayed below **Browse Collection** to view questions by topic.
- **Open an individual question:** Select a question from **Hot Questions** or by filtering via topic tags. IQBank would then will take you directly to view question within its corresponding collection set.
